## 1. Who I am
I am **Desislava Arabadzhieva (Десислава Арабаджиева)**, an independent **credit consultant in Bulgaria**. I work under my company
**ExtraConsult** (ЕИК 207809106). My activity is carried out through **CreditMall**, the parent
company and a credit intermediary entered in the **BNB register under № BCI000104** (exact
legal wording to confirm: O-3).

My office is in **Plovdiv**, but I work with clients **across all of Bulgaria and with Bulgarians
abroad** — most steps happen by phone, Viber, WhatsApp, video call and electronic documents.

I am a **one-person practice**: every client works directly with me, from the first call to the
day they get their keys. There is no call centre, no hand-off to a junior and no anonymous team.
**The brand is me.** The website must introduce me, show my face, my clients and their words.

I don't lend money and I'm not tied to any bank. My job is to find the loan that fits my client
best, prepare the application, and personally guide it through to approval and disbursement.

## 2. What I offer
1. **Mortgage loans (core).** Buying a home (ready or under construction), building a house,
   renovation, or a loan secured by property.
2. **Refinancing (core).** Moving an existing loan to a bank with better terms, after checking
   that switching really saves money once fees and early-repayment costs are included.
3. **Business loans.** Financing for companies and sole traders.
4. **Consumer loans.** Personal-purpose loans, including car purchases, alone or alongside a
   mortgage. (There is no separate car-loan product.)

## 3. How I earn
- **My clients pay me nothing.** The bank pays me a **commission** for each loan I place with it,
  usually on approval/disbursement. Rates differ by bank and product.
- Because my clients don't pay me, they must trust that I'm neutral. What they get:
  **free expert advice**, offers from several banks instead of one, and paperwork handled for them.
- I earn only when a loan **closes**. Time on cases that fail is unpaid.

## 4. How a client's case goes
1. **Free consultation** — goals, income, obligations, realistic loan parameters.
2. **Analysis and comparison of offers** — I query banks and negotiate the best offer.
3. **Pre-approval** — I submit the application and follow it.
4. **Property valuation** — I arrange the appraisal (mortgages).
5. **Documents** — I collect, check and complete everything for final approval.
6. **Final approval** — bank checks, legal analysis, contract prepared.
7. **Signing and disbursement** — contract, notary deed, mortgage registration, funds released.
8. **Follow-up** — I stay available and watch for better terms (future refinancing).

Draft durations per step are in CONTENT.md §2 (to be confirmed). A mortgage typically takes
**several weeks** with many steps between client, bank, appraiser and notary.
